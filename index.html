Voting rewards per week & max-stake APY

Reconstructed from the daily "last reward event" metric (approximate: when two rounds land on one day they're counted together). The steps down are reward-rate changes, the most recent from Mission 70.

APY = (rewards that week ÷ voting power of neurons eligible for rewards) × the voting-power multiplier of a max-staked neuron. Before 20 Apr 2026: 8-year dissolve delay (2×) plus the age bonus the oldest neurons could have had by then (up to 1.25× after 4 years). After Mission 70: 2-year max dissolve delay (3×) × 1.25 age. Assumes the neuron votes on everything and rewards are not compounded. Neurons flagged as "8-year gang" get roughly 10% more than shown.

Does history repeat? DFINITY's record with token holders

Written 14 Nov 2026 from the public documents linked at the end. It keeps what is documented apart from what is interpretation, and it doesn't claim to know anyone's intentions. It isn't financial advice.

What the record documents

  • The terms people staked under were changed by votes DFINITY proposed. The Mission 70 white paper (Jan 2026) cuts the maximum dissolve delay from 8 to 2 years for every neuron and scales voting rewards down by about 36%. Its only stated protection for people who locked for 8 years is a 10% reward boost that ends in 2030. The paper's own reason: long lock-ups need high rewards, which "contributes significantly to overall inflation" (section 4.1.2).
  • Passive holders mostly follow DFINITY by default. A review published by DFINITY in Feb 2023 reported that over 99% of voting power followed DFINITY or ICA neurons on non-governance topics, and that the two held 22.7% of voting power, down from about 39.5% just after genesis. An independent analyst counted DFINITY at 49.2% of the votes cast on a typical proposal in early 2022, and judged that a few parties could pass any proposal (his opinion).
  • The Neurons' Fund drew design objections. Community members argued it exposed participants to front-running and forced selling. DFINITY's engineer agreed with some points ("Details are not fully designed yet") and disagreed with others, and DFINITY later voted to reject a proposal to remove the Fund from the SNS launchpad, saying participants can opt out.

The other reading

The Mission 70 cap applies to every neuron, and cutting new issuance helps holders who don't stake. DFINITY's voting share has fallen over time, and its proposals don't always pass: the dissolve-delay-only proposal was rejected, a community counter-proposal to the Mission 70 motion was published, and the motion itself passed narrowly (see the table above). The documents show rule changes after people committed. They don't show that harming retail was the aim, and the stated aim is a 70% cut in inflation.

What would show history repeating, and what wouldn't

  • New terms for existing neurons. A proposal that changes lock length, the reward formula or conversion rules for neurons that already exist. Mission 70's 10% boost is the benchmark for what "compensation" looked like last time.
  • The 2030 boost. Whether the 8-year-gang bonus is kept to its end date, cut early or extended.
  • Rules on leaving. The paper suggests a dissolution queue, similar to Ethereum's validator queue, to smooth the one-time migration. A queue that delays when locked stake can exit would matter most when the 2-year cliff in the charts above arrives. Read its design before it is voted.
  • Who decides. The DFINITY and ICA voting share, how much power follows them by default, and the margins and turnout on each vote (Mission 70 passed with most voting power abstaining).
  • Disclosure. The paper says nothing about the Foundation's ICP holdings or treasury. That is a gap, not evidence of anything. Published holdings and sales would remove the uncertainty.

Reading. The documented pattern is that the rules retail staked under have changed more than once, each time through a vote DFINITY proposed, and the data above shows what that cost 8-year lockers and passive holders. The record doesn't settle whether the next change will repeat it. The five signs above are observable, so check them against each new proposal rather than relying on a forecast from this page.

Keeping it up to date

When opened from its own website this page pulls fresh numbers from these public APIs on every visit; inside Claude it shows a saved snapshot. No key is needed:

  • History: https://metrics-api.internetcomputer.org/api/v1/governance_total_maturity_e8s_equivalent?start=1692000000&end=NOW&step=86400
  • Staked maturity: …/api/v1/governance_total_staked_maturity_e8s_equivalent (same params)
  • Daily rewards: …/api/v1/last-reward-event (since Oct 2021)
  • Live snapshot with all slices: https://ic-api.internetcomputer.org/api/v3/governance-metrics, or the canister directly at https://rrkah-fqaaa-aaaaa-aaaaq-cai.raw.icp0.io/metrics

Values are in e8s (÷100,000,000 = ICP). Maturity converts to ICP at ±5% depending on maturity modulation at the time of spawn/disburse, so "ICP-equivalent" is approximate.
